## Brand & Style

The design system is grounded in editorial publishing heritage, tactile book collecting, and serene literary minimalism. Emulating an intimate physical archive and modern library shelves, it bridges tactile material culture with Android’s modern fluidity. The system addresses bibliophiles, collectors, and careful readers who view their digital catalog not as transactional database rows, but as an inhabited personal sanctuary.

The aesthetic philosophy draws directly from:
- **Literary Editorial Warmth:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with precise geometric sans-serif metadata, mimicking curated book jacket title pages and fine folio bindings.
- **Tactile Material Subtlety:** Quiet paper stocks, blind debossing, tactile spine textures, and fine hairline structural dividers reminiscent of archival catalog cards and binding stitchery.
- **Ornamental Typographic Restraint:** Selective deployment of classical printer's ornaments (asterisks `*`, micro plus signs `+`, geometric foils `○`, fine vertical rules, Roman numerals, and subtle border stamps) to anchor sections without graphic excess.
- **Shelf Rhythm:** Book spines arranged with vertical text flows, distinctive block widths, paper texture variances, and embossed foil stamping, transforming digital lists into personal library shelves.

## Colors

The palette reproduces warm bookbinding materials, archival papers, and printer inks:

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Base Screen Canvas: `#F2F0EA` (warm, unfiltered cream paper grain).
  - Surface Elevation 1 / Shelf Cards: `#FAF9F5` (crisp hot-pressed cotton paper).
  - Surface Inset / Recessed Shelves: `#EAE6DC` (aged parchment and warm raw board).
- **Ink & Typography:**
  - Primary Typographic Ink: `#202321` (deep carbon charcoal, softer than pure black to avoid digital glare).
  - Secondary Ink & Catalog Metadata: `#747872` (weathered slate grey for dates, publishers, and ISBN details).
  - Subtle Hairlines & Blind Rules: `#D8D4C8` (warm tactile linen thread).
- **Book Spine & Editorial Accents:**
  - Terracotta / Rust: `#B44D38` (traditional morocco leather and brick cloth bindings).
  - Forest Spruce Green: `#40584C` (deep archival cloth, botany plates, and cloth spine accents).
  - Deep Spine Charcoals: `#202321`, `#2C2F2D`, `#373D39` (heavy buckram cloth).
  - Spine Lettering Foils: `#F2F0EA` (cream foil on dark cloth) and `#202321` (ink stamped on parchment cloth).

## Typography

The typographic pairing honors classical bibliography while delivering functional Android mobile clarity:

- **Literata (Serif):** Employed for shelf titles, headers, author attributions, spine titles, and long-form reading passages. With its literary proportions, gentle diagonal stress, and deliberate ink traps, it gives the interface an authentic letterpress cadence.
- **IBM Plex Sans (Sans-Serif):** Engineered for navigational chrome, metadata rows, filter chips, catalog sorting, and system notifications. It provides an archival, typewritten counterweight to the warmth of the serif body.
- **Spine Orientations:** Book spine elements make use of vertical `writing-mode: vertical-rl` text orientation, accompanied by micro ornamental glyphs (`·`, `+`, `※`, `✦`) in `spine-meta`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an editorial catalog rhythm designed to balance dense shelf browsing with calm reading spaces:

- **Canvas Margins & Gutters:** Main screens use a 16px (`1rem`) margin on mobile, expanding to 24px (`1.5rem`) on tablets. Standard component gutters are 16px (`1rem`).
- **The Shelf Grid Model:** Physical book shelf racks rely on `gutter-shelf` (4px / `0.25rem`) spacing between vertical spines, maintaining continuous tactile grouping across the horizontal plane.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Standard content sections use 20px–28px vertical padding, separated by fine structural hairlines (`#D8D4C8`) with 8px clearance.
- **Tablet Reflow:** For larger viewports, the bookshelf viewport shifts from a horizontal swiper to an expansive dual-tier bookcase or a structured 3-column index card layout.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows and high-gloss lighting in favor of subtle print and bookbinding craft:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Debossing:** Cards and panels are defined by crisp, 1px low-contrast strokes (`#D8D4C8` or `rgba(32, 35, 33, 0.08)`) resembling blind stamping on book covers.
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is established by nesting surfaces: `#F2F0EA` as the table background, `#FAF9F5` as the raised archival card, and `#EAE6DC` as recessed shelf grooves.
- **Spine Micro-Shadows:** Shelf spine items use asymmetric lateral shadows (`inset -1px 0 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.12), inset 1px 0 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.25)`) to replicate the rounded cylinder of book spines and the shadow cast inside a bookshelf groove.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Android bottom sheets and contextual dialogs use a soft ambient shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(32, 35, 33, 0.08)`) combined with a solid 1px paper edge border.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, precision-cut paper geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Spine Elements:** Sharp 2px to 4px corners, mimicking cut pasteboard and trimmed book blocks.
- **Buttons & Filter Chips:** Soft 4px corner radii (`space-xs`) evoking classic library index tickets and library card pockets.
- **Modal Containers & Shelf Ledges:** 8px corner radii on bottom sheets, keeping the interface grounded and architectural.
- **Circular Badges & Ornaments:** Precise full circles (`rounded-full`) reserved strictly for typographic ornaments, count stamps, and icon buttons.

## Components

### Book Spine Unit (Core Product Anchor)
- **Geometry:** Upright vertical blocks with varied widths (36px to 64px) and heights (160px to 210px) to simulate realistic book volumes.
- **Palette Variants:**
  - Off-white/cream cloth (`#FAF9F5`) with `#202321` typography.
  - Parchment buff (`#EAE6DC`) with `#202321` typography.
  - Terracotta buckram (`#B44D38`) with `#F2F0EA` typography.
  - Deep spruce cloth (`#40584C`) with `#FAF9F5` typography.
  - Carbon charcoal cloth (`#202321`, `#2C2F2D`, `#373D39`) with cream or muted gold foil typography.
- **Details:** Vertical serif title running spine-length, debossed header/footer rules, publisher geometric stamp (`○`, `✦`, `※`), and simulated headband ribbing at the top edge.

### Shelf Shelfboard / Shelf Base
- **Geometry:** Full-width horizontal bar beneath spines.
- **Style:** 12px height in tone `#E4E0D5`, edged with a 1px top stroke `#D4CFC2` and a soft 2px downward gradient drop, grounding the row of books.

### Buttons & Editorial Actions
- **Primary Button:** `#202321` background, `#FAF9F5` typography, 4px border radius, 44px touch height, padded with `space-lg`. Hover/pressed states deepen to `#121413`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FAF9F5` with 1px border `#D8D4C8` and `#202321` typography.
- **Terracotta Accent Action:** `#B44D38` fill with `#FAF9F5` text for reading or priority archive flows.

### Segmented Tabs & Category Filters
- **Container:** Transparent background with bottom hairline `#D8D4C8`.
- **States:** Inactive tabs use `label-md` in `#747872`; active tabs use `label-md` in `#202321` with a 2px solid `#202321` underline and an optional micro asterisk `*` suffix indicator.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** `#FAF9F5` fill with a crisp 1px `#D8D4C8` border.
- **Typography:** `IBM Plex Sans` 14px text; placeholder in `#747872`.
- **Focus:** 1px border shift to `#202321` with zero fuzzy glow, preserving the clean paper aesthetic.

### Cards & Archival Meta Lists
- **Layout:** Two-column index card layout with book spine miniature on the left and title, author, reading progress, and ex-libris stamp on the right.
- **Dividers:** 1px dashed or solid hairline in `#E2DEC8`.
